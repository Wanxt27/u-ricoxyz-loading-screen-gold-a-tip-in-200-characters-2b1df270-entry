`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes exactly one loading-screen tip — 113 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Tip: if your build takes longer than your coffee break, it's not compiling — it's contemplating its life choices.

Every stressed developer has made the coffee-break comparison while staring at a slow build; the twist treats the compiler like a tired coworker contemplating its life choices. Short, sharp, zero wasted words.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
